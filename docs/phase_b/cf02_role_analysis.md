# Exact role of CF-02 (P5)

**Document:** Kailes & Enders (2007), *Moving Beyond “Special Needs”*, *Journal of Disability Policy Studies*  
**DOI:** 10.1177/10442073070170040601  
**Pilot role:** sole Phase-B benchmark comparator under PA-2026-07-31-001

## Why it was selected

1. Explicitly function-based AFN framing (anti-deficit; beyond undifferentiated “special needs”).
2. Conceptual origin point for CMIST-style functional domains in emergency planning discourse.
3. Provides a falsification / contrast anchor for RQ4 against school-security doctrine.
4. Named in the pilot sampling plan as the comparator pathway before operational CMIST L1 was pinned.

## Implication

The study does **not** require the same artefact for every role.  
It **does** require at least one authorized, PE-unitizable function-based / AFN operational L1 for Phase-B calibration and RQ4.

That L1 may be:

- CF-02 itself (OPT-A / OPT-C), once authorized; and/or  
- a formally amended operational document such as FEMA FNSS 2010 (OPT-B / OPT-C),

but **not** a silent substitute.
