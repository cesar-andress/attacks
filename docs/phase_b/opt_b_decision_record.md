## Scope decision

**Approach:** complete-document coding under the **existing L2 PE definition** (not outcome-selected chapter picking).

- **Include as candidate text:** entire authorized PDF (166 pp.).  
- **Unitize as PEs only:** statements that prescribe actions, mandate controls, or pose assessment questions such that a planner/operator could comply or not.  
- **Exclude as PEs (by existing rules):** pure purpose/disclaimer without actionable content; bare definitions; bibliographic lists; non-prescriptive narrative examples (examples may be memos; do not auto-elevate rung).  
- **Appendices / tools:** eligible if they contain assessment checklists or operational requirements meeting the PE definition.  
- **Denominator:** all eligible PEs unitized from the document under those rules.

No chapter whitelist selected by anticipated high engagement.

## Late-access rule for CF-02

**RULE-LATE-CF02 (hybrid 3→2):**

1. If authorized CF-02 full text becomes available **before** independent Phase-B **target** coding begins, a further amendment may authorize dual-benchmark (OPT-C) before coding starts.  
2. If access occurs **after** Phase-B target coding has begun, CF-02 may be coded later only as a **supplementary** calibration (does not replace or redefine the primary operational comparator FNSS; does not rewrite preregistered primary RQ4 denominator).  
3. Post-result expansion of the primary comparator set is **prohibited**.
