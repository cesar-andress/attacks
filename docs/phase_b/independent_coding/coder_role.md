# Independent coder — role description (public-safe)

**Status:** recruitment materials only — **no coder recruited**  
**Study:** RP-P1 documentary audit (protective-action executability engagement)  
**Comparator context:** FEMA FNSS 2010 is the operational Phase-B L1 under amendment `PA-2026-07-31-002`; CF-02 is conceptual only. The comparator is contrastive, not a gold standard.

## Task purpose

Apply the published codebook independently to unitize and code authorized documentary text so that intercoder reliability and benchmark calibration can be assessed under preregistered rules.

## Expected time commitment

- Training + qualification: approximately 4–8 hours.  
- Phase-B target coding (single operational L1): plan for 1–3 working days after qualification (depends on PE yield).  
- Later corpus double-coding: per the reliability policy (full double-coding if the corpus is small).

Exact scheduling: `TO_BE_SET_BY_HUMAN`.

## Required skills

Any one of the following is sufficient:

- content analysis / qualitative coding experience; or  
- emergency management / school safety practice; or  
- accessibility / functional-needs / inclusive education familiarity;  

plus ability to follow written coding rules over personal preference, and to read English (plus French/Spanish if those sources are later assigned).

## Independence requirements

- No prior involvement in development of this study’s codebook.  
- No role in selecting the Phase-B comparator.  
- No role in Phase-A adjudication of the same units.  
- No access to target codes, BDD adjudication outcomes, or qualification answer keys before submission.  
- No viewing of another coder’s values before raw submission lock.  
- No outside consultation on case answers during independent coding.  
- No authority to change the codebook; propose memos only through approved channels.

## Confidentiality

Follow the confidentiality agreement. Controlled project materials must not be redistributed. Public source doctrine may be cited under its own terms; project extracts and coding files remain controlled.

## Uncertainty

Record uncertainty only with approved fields (`ambiguous_memo`, procedural notes, uncodeable states). Do not invent evidence.
