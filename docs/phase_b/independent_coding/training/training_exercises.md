# Brief training exercises (no answer key)

**Access:** PRE_QUALIFICATION_SAFE  
Work these privately; discuss only process questions with the PI (see procedural-query protocol). Do not request correct codes before qualification.

1. **Unitization:** Split or justify a single PE for: “Staff must lock the door and silence devices.”  
2. **Ladder:** Distinguish support specification from assessment language.  
3. **Demand source:** Classify “remain silent” vs “consider sensory needs where appropriate.”  
4. **Setting vs support:** Decide whether a facility catalog alone licenses `support_present=1`.  
5. **Claims:** Restate why documentary codes do not prove measure effectiveness.

Cross-check your reasoning against the training guide and public codebook — not against expected study results.
