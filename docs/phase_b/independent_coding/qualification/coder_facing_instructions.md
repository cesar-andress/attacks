# Qualification — coder instructions

1. Work alone; do not consult answer keys or other people about case answers.  
2. Use only the codebook, training guide, and the qualification stimuli.  
3. Complete every `case_id` in the response CSV.  
4. Leave uncertainty as `ambiguous_memo` or blank fields per schema — do not guess to force presence.  
5. Submit the CSV through the PI’s designated channel.  
6. After submission, do not alter your file; the PI locks a hashed copy.

You will receive a pass/fail decision after scoring and human review. Do not assume readiness from an informal conversation.
