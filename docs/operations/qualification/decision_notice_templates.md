# Qualification decision notices (templates)

## Pass / proceed to READY checklist

You passed the qualification exercise for set [SET]. This does not by itself open sealed materials. Next steps follow the activation checklist.

## Retry (attempt 2 on a different set)

You did not meet the qualification threshold on attempt 1. Under QUAL-ATTEMPT-v1 you may make one further valid attempt on a **different** qualification set. Answer keys are not provided.

## Final failure

You did not meet the qualification standard after the maximum valid attempts. Restricted materials will not be released. Please delete local copies of qualification packages and confirm.
