# Secure handling instructions (coder-facing summary)

**Not legal advice.** Institutional review may be required.

## You may receive

- Copyright-restricted guidance PDFs (local use only)  
- Training and qualification packages  
- After READY: sealed target coding materials  

## You must

- Store files only on approved devices under your control  
- Use full-disk encryption where available  
- Keep accounts password-protected  
- Report loss/theft within 24 hours  

## You must not

- Forward restricted files  
- Upload corpus/qualification/target documents to public AI systems  
- Sync restricted folders to personal public cloud shares  
- Discuss coding cases with others before reliability calculation  
- Request or seek answer keys  

## Return / destruction

On withdrawal, failure, or study close: delete local copies of restricted materials and confirm in writing to the PI. Public Zenodo materials may be retained.
