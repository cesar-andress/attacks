# Conflict-of-interest decision guide (independent coder)

**Companion form:** `docs/phase_b/independent_coding/forms/independence_coi_form.md`

## Automatically disqualifying (default)

- Contributed to RP-P1 codebook, ladder, or manuscript Methods for this paper.  
- Authored or edited a target L1 document under coding.  
- Previously saw qualification answer keys for the active set.  
- Previously saw sealed target codes for units to be coded.  
- Paid advocacy role requiring a particular coding outcome.

## Potentially manageable (escalate)

- Distant institutional affiliation with no document authorship.  
- Prior general consulting in school safety unrelated to target texts.  
- Prior student relationship with PI not involving this study.

Mitigations may include exclusion from specific documents, dual independent review, or declining the candidate.

## Non-material (usually proceed)

- General interest in school safety.  
- Reading public guidance as a practitioner without authorship.  
- Shared university without role on this project.

## Escalation

Document the disclosure, proposed mitigation, and decision in the private admin folder. Public dashboard records only: COI cleared / not cleared / pending, with coded candidate ID.
