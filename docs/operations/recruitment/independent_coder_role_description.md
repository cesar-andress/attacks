# Independent coder role description

**Study:** RP-P1 documentary audit of school-security / emergency-preparedness guidance.  
**Measured construct:** executability engagement (documentary), not real-world performance.  
**Status:** recruitment template (no candidate selected).

## Purpose

Apply the published codebook independently to unitize and code authorized documentary text so that intercoder reliability and Phase-B calibration can be assessed under preregistered rules.

## Tasks

1. Complete orientation, training, and confidentiality/independence steps.  
2. Complete qualification under `QUAL-ATTEMPT-v1` (max two valid attempts).  
3. If qualified: independently unitize and code authorized calibration / corpus documents.  
4. Submit locked coding files; respond only to procedural clarifications.  
5. Do not adjudicate; do not rewrite the codebook.

## Phases

1. Screening and onboarding  
2. Training  
3. Qualification  
4. Calibration coding (after READY)  
5. Possible later corpus double-coding (after freeze)

## Eligibility

No predefined academic degree, professional credential, or subject-matter expertise is required. Eligibility is determined by independence, confidentiality compliance, COI clearance, completed training, and successful qualification. Helpful (not required): content analysis, emergency management, school safety, or accessibility familiarity. English reading required for assigned English texts.

## Independence and conflicts

No prior involvement in RP-P1 codebook/manuscript development for target units; no prior access to qualification answer keys or sealed target codes; no concurrent case consultation. See COI form and decision guide.

## Technical requirements

Secure personal or institutional computer; ability to work with CSV/Markdown packages offline; no upload of restricted documents to public AI systems.

## Confidentiality

Copyright-restricted guidance PDFs, qualification materials, and sealed targets must not be forwarded, posted, or reused outside the study. See confidentiality agreement.

## Training and qualification

Training package TRAIN-v1 must be completed before qualification. Qualification uses frozen `QUAL-SET-v1` / `QUAL-ATTEMPT-v1`: maximum two valid attempts; no answer-key disclosure; answer-key exposure invalidates the set; technical failures documented may not consume an attempt.

## Compensation and recruitment channel

**Channel:** direct personal or professional contact (not an open call).  
**Compensation:** voluntary, non-remunerated collaboration (no payment).  
Participation is not contingent on pass/fail, coding results, reliability estimates, or manuscript conclusions (UCW-003 RESOLVED).

## Communication boundaries

Procedural questions only through the approved channel. No case-level answer shopping. No discussion of coding cases with other coders before reliability calculation.

## Withdrawal and replacement

A candidate may withdraw in writing. Replacement requires a new screened candidate and full onboarding; prior sealed materials remain sealed; compromised qualification sets are not reused for the exposed person.
