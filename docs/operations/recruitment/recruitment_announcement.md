# Recruitment announcement: independent documentary coder (RP-P1)

We seek an independent coder for a documentary content analysis of school-security and emergency-preparedness **guidance documents**.

This role codes **text**, not schools, pupils, or real-world incident outcomes. It does not evaluate whether measures “work.”

## What you would do

- Train on a written codebook.  
- Complete a qualification exercise (up to two valid attempts).  
- If qualified, independently code authorized documents using the same rules.  
- Submit coding files for reliability assessment.

## Time commitment

- Training + qualification: about 4-8 hours.  
- Calibration coding after qualification: plan 1-3 working days (document-dependent).  
- Later double-coding may follow if the corpus remains small.

Exact scheduling: Decision pending (UCW-003).

## Compensation

Compensation terms: **Decision pending** (UCW-003). Payment, if any, is not contingent on qualification pass/fail or on producing a preferred reliability result.

## Requirements

- Ability to apply written coding rules consistently.  
- Independence from the study’s codebook development and sealed materials.  
- Completion of conflict-of-interest screening and confidentiality agreement.  
- Secure local handling of restricted documents (no public AI upload of corpus files).

## Confidentiality

Some materials are copyright-restricted and confidential to the study. Do not redistribute.

## How to apply

Contact the study lead using the channel listed when UCW-003 is resolved. Submit a brief statement of relevant experience and availability. Do not request answer keys or sealed documents.

## Non-goals

- Not an evaluation of schools or occupants.  
- Not advocacy coding.  
- Not Branch B / threat-detectability coding in this paper.
