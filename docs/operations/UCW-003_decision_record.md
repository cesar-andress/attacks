## Rationale

The study evaluates the reproducibility of the documented coding protocol. The protocol is intended to be executable by an independent trained coder rather than requiring specialist expertise in school security.

Direct recruitment is appropriate given the limited number of coders required.

Voluntary participation avoids introducing performance-dependent incentives while maintaining coder independence.

## Non-contingent participation principle

Participation must **not** depend on qualification pass/fail, coding results, reliability estimates, or manuscript conclusions. (There is no payment contingent on outcomes.)
