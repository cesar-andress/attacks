# Five-year roadmap (limited resources; not guaranteed dates)

**Assumption:** constrained researcher capacity; CF-02/benchmark access may delay Year 1; external funding not assumed.

| Year | Required | Desirable | Funding-dependent |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Resolve sources; complete documentary pilot (incl. Phase B); freeze corpus; preregister; complete RP-P1 coding; submit RP-P1; keep preliminary SLAPA architecture maintained | Early expert conversations for RP-P2 | None required |
| **2** | Revise RP-P1 through peer review; RP-P2 expert review + content validity; begin cognitive interviews | Start RP-P4 documentary module if capacity | Delphi platform support; travel |
| **3** | Finish/submit RP-P2; small feasibility pilot; prepare RP-P3 sampling + ethics | Soft policy briefing notes (non-claiming) | Ethics staff time; school recruitment incentives |
| **4** | Collect RP-P3 field data; reliability/validity analyses; submit RP-P4 if pursued | First intervention design memo | Field data collection budget |
| **5** | Submit RP-P3; practice manual (claims-bounded); prepare intervention studies; prepare Horizon/policy synthesis | Multi-country extension | Horizon Europe or equivalent |

## Sequencing rule

Later years **do not unlock** if Year 1 RP-P1 methodological execution remains incomplete. Programme documentation must not substitute for RP-P1 completion.

## Non-implications

- “Submit” ≠ acceptance.  
- Roadmap entries are plans, not completed work.  
- RP-P4 remains Priority 4 (capacity-gated).
