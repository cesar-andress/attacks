# Evidence-layer model

**Purpose:** Prevent documentary variables from being read as real-world preparedness or effectiveness.

## Layers

| Layer | Name | Question | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Doctrinal content | What does authoritative guidance prescribe/engage? | National/agency frameworks; RP-P1 corpus |
| **2** | Local adoption | Has the centre adopted/adapted relevant provisions? | Local plans; role appointments; policy indexes |
| **3** | Organisational capability | Are people, supports, authority, resources, procedures actually available? | Staffing; equipment; supports; decision rights |
| **4** | Exercised performance | What happens in drills, simulations, controlled exercises? | Observed drills; REX; timed routes; inclusive adaptations |
| **5** | Real-world outcome | What happens in actual incidents? | Incident investigations; after-action; outcome evaluations |

## Paper mapping

| Paper | Primary layers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **RP-P1** | **Layer 1 only** | Executability engagement in doctrine |
| **RP-P2** | Develops measures for **Layers 2–4** | Architecture/content validity; not field-validated |
| **RP-P3** | Validates mainly **Layers 2–4** | Reliability/construct validity |
| **RP-P4** | Layer 1 module and/or later 2–4 Domain B | Independent of demand–capability spine |

## Explicit prohibitions

- No planned study in this programme may claim **Layer-5 effectiveness** without appropriate incident or intervention evidence.  
- Layer 1 codes must not be reported as Layer 3 capability or Layer 4 performance.  
- Local document presence (Layer 2) must not be reported as Layer 4 performance.

## Analytical reminder

Document content ≠ organisational adoption ≠ organisational capability ≠ individual perception ≠ observed performance.
