# Validation roadmap (RP-P2 / RP-P3)

**Instrument status throughout:** unvalidated until Stage 8+ criteria are met and reported with claims bounds.

## Stage boundary (recommended)

| Paper | Stages |
|---|---|
| **RP-P2** | 1–5 (and optionally Stage 6 small feasibility pilot) |
| **RP-P3** | 6 (expanded) / 7–10 |

---

### STAGE 1 — Construct specification
- **Purpose:** Lock domain definitions and non-goals.  
- **Participants:** Core research team.  
- **Data:** RP-P1 findings; theory; architecture docs.  
- **Decision criteria:** Domains A–H defined; B separated from D; no single score.  
- **Risks:** Scope creep into Paper 1.  
- **Output:** Frozen construct memo for RP-P2.  
- **Dependency:** RP-P1 substantive findings available (not Phase A pilot alone).

### STAGE 2 — Evidence mapping
- **Purpose:** Map each domain to Layer 2–4 observables.  
- **Participants:** Core team + optional practitioner advisors.  
- **Data:** Traceability matrix; evidence-layer model.  
- **Decision criteria:** Every candidate indicator has layer tag and prohibited inference.  
- **Risks:** Direct PE→item translation.  
- **Output:** Evidence map.  
- **Dependency:** Stage 1.

### STAGE 3 — Expert review / Delphi
- **Purpose:** Relevance/clarity of domains and evidence types.  
- **Participants:** School security, AFN/inclusion, special education, leadership, police/BTAM (for B only).  
- **Data:** Structured ratings/comments.  
- **Decision criteria:** Pre-set consensus thresholds.  
- **Risks:** Security-only panel ignoring inclusion.  
- **Output:** Revised architecture.  
- **Dependency:** Stage 2.

### STAGE 4 — Content-validity assessment
- **Purpose:** Quantify content validity (e.g. CVI) where appropriate.  
- **Participants:** Expert panel.  
- **Data:** Item/indicator relevance ratings (still not a finished questionnaire claim).  
- **Decision criteria:** Pre-registered CVI cut-offs.  
- **Risks:** Treating CVI as construct validity.  
- **Output:** Content-validity report (RP-P2).  
- **Dependency:** Stage 3.

### STAGE 5 — Cognitive interviews
- **Purpose:** Comprehension of organisational prompts by intended respondents.  
- **Participants:** Heads; security leads; SENCOs/inclusion leads.  
- **Data:** Interview transcripts/notes.  
- **Decision criteria:** Major ambiguities resolved or flagged.  
- **Risks:** Overfitting to one jurisdiction.  
- **Output:** Wording revisions.  
- **Dependency:** Stage 4.

### STAGE 6 — Small field pilot / feasibility
- **Purpose:** Feasibility of administration; not confirmatory validation.  
- **Participants:** Few centres.  
- **Data:** Pilot responses; process metrics.  
- **Decision criteria:** Completion burden acceptable; no silent promotion to validated.  
- **Risks:** Overclaiming from n-small.  
- **Output:** Feasibility memo (end of RP-P2 or start RP-P3).  
- **Dependency:** Stage 5.

### STAGE 7 — Reliability testing
- **Purpose:** Internal consistency / test–retest / inter-rater as design dictates.  
- **Participants:** RP-P3 sample.  
- **Data:** Field scores.  
- **Decision criteria:** Pre-specified reliability bands.  
- **Risks:** Reliability without validity.  
- **Output:** Reliability tables (RP-P3).  
- **Dependency:** Stage 6 + ethics/sampling.

### STAGE 8 — Construct validation
- **Purpose:** Structural/convergent/divergent evidence for domain profile.  
- **Participants:** RP-P3 sample.  
- **Data:** Field scores; planned correlates (not incident counts as primary criterion).  
- **Decision criteria:** Pre-specified model expectations.  
- **Risks:** Forcing unidimensionality / total score.  
- **Output:** Construct-validity report.  
- **Dependency:** Stage 7.

### STAGE 9 — Group comparison / measurement invariance
- **Purpose:** Compare organisational conditions across setting types with safeguards.  
- **Participants:** Inclusive mainstream / special education / others if justified.  
- **Data:** Domain profiles; invariance tests if sample allows.  
- **Decision criteria:** Comparisons concern supports/constraints/coordination — not inherent safety ranks.  
- **Risks:** Stigmatising special education.  
- **Output:** Safeguarded comparison results.  
- **Dependency:** Stage 8; `comparison_safeguards.md`.

### STAGE 10 — Manual and claims-policy development
- **Purpose:** User manual + permitted claims by validation stage achieved.  
- **Participants:** Team + practitioner review.  
- **Data:** Stages 1–9 outputs.  
- **Decision criteria:** Aligns with `future_claims_policy.md`.  
- **Risks:** Marketing overclaim.  
- **Output:** Manual v1; claims matrix.  
- **Dependency:** Stage 8 minimum; Stage 9 if comparisons published.
