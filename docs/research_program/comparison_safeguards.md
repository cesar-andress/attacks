# Future comparison logic and ethical/analytical safeguards

## Permitted comparison objects

Future studies (chiefly RP-P3) may compare educational settings such as:

- mainstream schools;  
- inclusive mainstream schools;  
- special education settings;  
- other educational settings if sampling justification is recorded.

Comparisons must concern **organisational and environmental conditions**, for example:

- dependencies (staffing ratios, specialist presence, transport);  
- available supports and access provisions;  
- evacuation and lockdown constraints;  
- communication requirements and modalities;  
- staffing models;  
- environmental / security-by-design characteristics;  
- interagency coordination arrangements;  
- exercise design (including inclusive adaptations);  
- organisational learning routines.

## Explicit non-presumptions (safeguards)

The programme **must not** presume or imply:

1. that special education pupils are incapable of serious violence;  
2. that threats in special education settings are exclusively external;  
3. that special education schools are inherently softer targets;  
4. that one school category is categorically safer;  
5. that disability status is a proxy for dangerousness or for vulnerability-as-deficit.

## Reporting rules

- Report **domain profiles** and condition contrasts, not league tables of school types.  
- Prefer language about *supports, constraints, and operating conditions* over language about *pupil deficits*.  
- Any group difference requires a construct-valid interpretation plan and, where feasible, measurement-invariance checks.  
- Absence of differentiated doctrine in RP-P1 must not be narrated as proof that a setting type is unsafe.
