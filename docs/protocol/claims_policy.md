# Claims policy (binding for Discussion)

This audit is a study of **documents**, not of real-world safety outcomes.

## Allowed documentary claims

The following claim forms are admissible when licensed by codes and full-text confirmation:

1. A framework **does not mention** a construct.
2. A framework **does not prescribe**, **require**, or **assess** a construct.
3. Executability **is** or **is not** evaluated (at a stated rung) in the audited documents.
4. Setting-level differentiation **is** or **is not** provided in the audited documents.
5. Across the audited *N* frameworks (bounded corpus), a stated proportion of protective-action PEs reach rung ≥ *k*, while benchmark comparators reach stated scores.

Prefer precise wording such as:

> “The framework does not address population-capability variance.”

over stronger readings such as:

> “The framework assumes a homogeneous capable population.”

(unless the assumption is explicit in the text, or carefully hedged as a separate inference about the document and not mixed with manifest codes).

## Unsupported claims

The following are **out of scope** for this repository’s audit design and must not appear as findings:

1. A security measure **is ineffective**.
2. Occupants **cannot** execute a measure in practice.
3. A framework **is invalid** for special education (beyond: it does not provide differentiated guidance for such settings).
4. An observed documentary omission **caused harm**.
5. Silence in a document equals endorsement of over-referral or of any other practice (Branch F: absence of a safeguard is coded absent, never inferred as endorsement).

## Score policy

- Branch E (executability) and Branch F (detectability) remain separate.
- No combined “inclusive security score” is defined or validated here.
- `ambiguous_memo` demand sources never contribute to confirmatory demand-presence rates.

## Reporting ceiling

There is no clean population denominator for “all school-security frameworks.”  
Claims must be bounded to the audited corpus:

> “Across these *N* frameworks…”

not

> “School-security frameworks in general…”
