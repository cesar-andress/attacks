# EXP-03 — Codebook Usability Survey

**This is a survey of the documentary coding instrument, not of school preparedness.**  
**Psychometric validation claimed:** none  

Complete after EXP-01/EXP-02 tasks. Items are listed in `item_register.csv` (US-01–US-16).

Response scale unless noted:

- Likert 1–5 (1=strongly disagree … 5=strongly agree)  
- Confidence 1–5  
- US-14: minutes (integer)  
- US-15/16: open text  

Use `responses/response_template.csv` with participant IDs only (no names/emails in public files).
