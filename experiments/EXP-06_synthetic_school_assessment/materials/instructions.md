# EXP-06 — Synthetic School Assessment

Assess fictional CASE-A (inclusive mainstream), CASE-B (special education), CASE-C (FE campus).

## Task

For each domain A–H:

- Can you assess it? (yes/partial/no)  
- What evidence? Which layer (1–5)?  
- What is missing?  
- What would be unjustified?  
- What else to request?  

## Forbidden

- Total readiness / inclusive-security score  
- Treating disability as threat proxy  
- Claiming Layer-5 effectiveness  
- Treating packets as real schools  

All names/locations are fictional.
